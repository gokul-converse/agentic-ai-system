HR Policy Manual – Core Policies 2025
Last updated: January 2025

1. Purpose & Scope
This document outlines the key human resources policies applicable to all employees of Apex Innovations Pvt Ltd (the "Company"). These policies are designed to ensure a fair, safe, respectful and productive work environment.

All employees, contractors, interns and consultants are expected to read, understand and comply with these policies. Violation may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

2. Working Hours & Attendance
• Standard work week: Monday–Friday
• Core working hours: 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM (including 1 hour lunch break)
• Flexible start time allowed between 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM (with manager approval)
• Employees must maintain minimum 40 productive hours per week (excluding lunch)
• Remote work: Allowed up to 3 days/week after completion of probation (subject to manager approval)
• Attendance tracking: Biometric + self-reported timesheet in HRMS (Zoho People / equivalent)

Late arrival policy:
• 0–15 min late: considered grace (max 3 times/month)
• 16–60 min late: 0.5 day leave deduction or salary deduction
• >60 min late: full day deduction unless pre-approved

4. Code of Conduct & Workplace Behaviour
4.1 Anti-Harassment & POSH Policy
The Company maintains zero tolerance for sexual harassment, bullying, discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, age, disability, marital status, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic.

All complaints will be investigated within 30–45 days as per the POSH Act 2013. Retaliation against complainant is strictly prohibited.

4.2 Gifts & Conflicts of Interest
Employees shall not accept gifts > ₹5,000 from vendors/clients without disclosure to HR.  
Any personal/family financial interest in a vendor/client must be declared annually.

4.3 Confidentiality & Data Protection
All company data, client information, source code, financials, strategies are strictly confidential.  
Employees must sign NDA on Day 1. Breach may result in civil & criminal action.

4.4 Social Media & Public Statements
Employees must not post anything that:
- damages company reputation
- discloses confidential information
- appears to speak on behalf of the company (unless authorized)

5. Dress Code & Grooming
• Monday–Thursday: Business casual / Smart casual
• Friday: Casual (no shorts, sleeveless, torn clothing, flip-flops)
• Client-facing meetings / interviews: Formal business attire
• Safety-critical areas (lab/server room): Closed shoes mandatory

6. Performance & Increments
• Annual appraisal cycle: April–March
• Increments usually communicated in May–June
• Performance rating scale: 1–5 (5 = Exceptional)
• Promotion eligibility: minimum 18 months in current role + consistent “4” or above rating

7. Termination & Notice Period
Category              Notice Period         Garden Leave possible?
---------------------------------------------------------------
Probation (0–6 months)       15 days              No
Confirmed employee           60 days              Yes (up to 60 days)
Manager & above              90 days              Yes
Termination for cause         Immediate           No notice / severance

Full & final settlement will be processed within 45 days of last working day after receiving all company assets.

8. Remote Work & Hybrid Policy (2025)
• Eligible after 6 months of service
• Maximum 60% remote work (3 days/week)
• Must be available during core hours 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
• Must attend office for:
  - All-hands meetings
  - Client visits
  - Team onboarding/offsite
  - Performance reviews (at least quarterly)
• Stable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps) is employee responsibility

9. Employee Benefits – Summary
• Group Health Insurance: ₹5 lakh coverage (employee + spouse + 2 children)
• Personal Accident Insurance: ₹10 lakh
• Term Life Insurance: 36× monthly CTC
• Mediclaim top-up available (employee contribution)
• Provident Fund + Gratuity as per statute
• Annual Health Check-up (₹3,000 reimbursement)
• Learning & Development: ₹15,000/year reimbursement (approved courses)

10. Disciplinary Procedure
Step 1: Verbal warning + documented discussion
Step 2: Written warning
Step 3: Final written warning + Performance Improvement Plan (30–90 days)
Step 4: Termination

Immediate termination possible for: theft, fraud, sexual harassment, violence, gross insubordination, sharing confidential data externally, intoxication at workplace.

11. Grievance Redressal
Employees can raise grievance to:
1. Immediate Manager
2. HR Business Partner
3. Grievance Committee (comprising CHRO + 2 senior leaders)

Anonymous reporting channel available via email: ethics@apexinnovations.in
